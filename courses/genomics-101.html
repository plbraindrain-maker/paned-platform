Course Overview

This course introduces the foundations of genomics in clear, accessible language while connecting learners to real-world applications in medicine, biotechnology, cancer research, sequencing, and artificial intelligence.

Who Should Take This Course?

Students, healthcare professionals, biotechnology learners, patients, career changers, educators, and anyone curious about genomics.

Recommended Background

No formal prerequisites required. Basic biology knowledge such as cells, DNA, genes, and proteins is recommended.

What You Will Earn

A PanorOmics Online completion certificate after completing all modules and passing the final assessment.

What You Will Learn

Define Genomics

Understand genomics and how it differs from genetics.

Understand Genome Structure

Learn about DNA, genes, chromosomes, and non-coding regions.

Explore Sequencing

Compare Sanger sequencing, next-generation sequencing, and long-read sequencing.

Connect to Medicine

See how genomics supports precision medicine, cancer treatment, and pharmacogenomics.

Meet AI Biology

Discover how AI and foundation models are changing genomics and biotechnology.

Prepare for Advanced Study

Build a foundation for OSC-710 Genomics and future advanced omics courses.
