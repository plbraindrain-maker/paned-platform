Genomics 101

GEN-101

GEN-101 is a free introductory genomics course for complete beginners. In this course, learners move from understanding what a genome is to seeing how genomics is transforming medicine, cancer research, sequencing technologies, precision medicine, and AI biology.

FORMATSelf-paced
TIME COMMITMENT3–4 hours
LEVELBeginner
CERTIFICATECompletion certificate

Course Overview

This course introduces the foundations of genomics in plain language while connecting learners to real-world applications in medicine, biotechnology, cancer genomics, sequencing, and artificial intelligence. It is designed as a free entry point into the PanorOmics Online genomics learning pathway.

Who Should Take This Course?

Students, healthcare professionals, biotechnology learners, patients, career changers, and anyone curious about genomics.

What You Will Earn

A PanorOmics Online completion certificate after passing the final assessment.

Learning Pathway

GEN-101 introduces learners to future paid courses in genomics, cancer genomics, and advanced AI-driven genomics.

What You Will Learn

Define Genomics

Understand genomics and how it differs from genetics.

Understand Genome Structure

Learn about DNA, genes, chromosomes, and non-coding regions.

Explore Sequencing

Compare Sanger sequencing, next-generation sequencing, and long-read sequencing.

Connect to Medicine

See how genomics supports precision medicine, cancer treatment, and pharmacogenomics.

Meet AI Biology

Discover AlphaFold2, DNA language models, and the future of genomics in the AI era.

Prepare for Advanced Study

Build a foundation for paid genomics, cancer genomics, and advanced omics courses.

Course Modules

Module 1 — What is Genomics?

3 Lessons

Introduces the big picture of genomics and why the Human Genome Project changed science.

  • Genomics — The Big Picture
  • A Brief History of Genomics
  • Why Genomics Matters — For You

Module 2 — Genes, Genomes, and Chromosomes

3 Lessons

Builds a clear mental model of how genetic information is organized.

  • The Genome — Size, Structure, and Scope
  • What is a Gene?
  • Reading the Genome — Databases and Reference Sequences

Module 3 — DNA Structure and Gene Expression

3 Lessons

Introduces DNA structure, the central dogma, and gene regulation.

  • The Double Helix — DNA Structure
  • From DNA to Protein — The Central Dogma
  • Gene Regulation — Not All Genes Are On All the Time

Module 4 — Genetic Variation

3 Lessons

Explains human genetic variation and how variants relate to health and disease.

  • Types of Genetic Variation
  • How Variation Causes Disease
  • Finding Disease Variants — GWAS and Variant Databases

Module 5 — Sequencing Technologies

3 Lessons

Shows how scientists read DNA using Sanger, NGS, and long-read sequencing.

  • Sanger Sequencing — The Original Method
  • Next-Generation Sequencing — The Revolution
  • Long-Read Sequencing and the Future

Module 6 — Genomics and Precision Medicine

3 Lessons

Connects genomics to cancer treatment, rare disease diagnosis, and medications.

  • What is Precision Medicine?
  • Cancer Genomics — Reading Tumor DNA
  • Pharmacogenomics — Your DNA and Your Medications

Module 7 — Genomics in the AI Biology Era

3 Lessons

Introduces AlphaFold2, DNA language models, foundation models, and future genomics technologies.

  • AlphaFold2 — AI Solves the Protein Folding Problem
  • DNA Language Models and Foundation Models for Biology
  • The Future of Genomics — Where Is This Going?
